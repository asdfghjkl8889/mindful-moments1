Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email address. You'll be asked your security question, and then you can set a new password right away — no email required.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your wellness data is stored locally on your device first. If you have an account, it is securely backed up to our servers using HTTPS encryption. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Create a free account (or log in if you already have one) on each device. Your moods, journal entries, meditation history, goals, and all other progress will automatically sync when you log in and when you background the app.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, email us at support@mindfulmoments.app with the subject line "Delete my account" and we will remove everything within 48 hours.

The app crashed or isn't loading — what do I do?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall it from the App Store — your data is safely backed up to your account and will be fully restored the moment you log back in.

How do I cancel or manage my account?

Mindful Moments is completely free with no subscription. There is nothing to cancel. Simply delete the app if you no longer wish to use it, or email us to remove your account data.
